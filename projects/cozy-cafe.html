Overview

'Cozy Cafe' Was our 2nd to last masters project. We were in a team 5.

It was nice to be in a team based environment again and allowed me to play more in the "art-sy" side of programming. Such as the loading screens, camera manipulation and dialogue systems

My Involvement

The character controller with leaning to velocity.

The camera manipulation system. Uses a stack reservation system, meaning if an entity wants to claim the camera, it'll claim possession over it. As soon as it relieves possession, it'll switch back to the previous entity using the selected profile.

The interaction system and all the various interactions in the game other than the associated crafting system interactions.

The dialogue system and its decision tree elements.

Most of the UI functionality.
