Overview

'Sky High' was the last project during my masters course. I collaborated with Matt Roberts who assisted in making this with me.

I took advantage of the steering behaviours used in 'Reclaim our lands'. Fixed it up and also used A* for navigation.

My Involvement

The bird controller + procedural animations using rotational maths on the armature of the bird

My implementation of A* which I like to label my "Unlocked A* Pathfinding" Because its grid based but not bound to a typically defined grid. It uses world space and simply branches out. This implementation can theoretically could go infinitely in any direction. However, to keep it in check there is a "max iterations" variable to keep it bound.

The weighted steering behaviours stacked ontop of A* to mimic flock behaviours and obstacle avoidance.
